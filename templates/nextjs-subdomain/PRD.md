## Scope Of This Document

This PRD governs **`[product].typhed.com` alone**, the property this repository builds. Every TyPhed property keeps its
own requirements document in its own repository: `typhed.com` shapes the brand layer, and each product shapes its own
product. Nothing written here constrains another property, and a decision made elsewhere does not reach back into this
document.

What the properties share is the brand, not the requirements. The shared layer carries the brand contract, the design
tokens, and the [domain and subdomain model](../shared/documents/docs/brand/subdomain-model.md), which records how the
ecosystem is laid out across domains, repositories, and hosts.
